How Lumo works

1

Your child shares emotions

Every day Lumo asks 2–3 simple prompts: “What was the happiest moment today?” “Was there anything that made you feel sad?” Kids can answer with a voice note, emoji, or drawing.

2

AI reflects with care

Lumo reads the mood, spots repeating themes, and builds an emotional map that grows with your child.

3

Parents receive guidance

Each week Lumo shares a gentle report with tips so you can open conversations, celebrate wins, and notice signals early.
